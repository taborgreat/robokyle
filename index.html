How it works

Adaptive equipment costs a fortune and plenty of it is simple to make. Anyone who builds something that helps can put the files up here, so the next person starts from a working design instead of from nothing.

  • Photos, printable files and the build guide all live on the same page.
  • Download whatever you need. No account, no cost.
  • Ask in the comments if a step is unclear. Whoever built it can answer.
  • Improved it? Post your version. Every edit is kept, so nothing gets lost.

This started with a friend, an accident, and the price of the equipment he needed. Read the story.
