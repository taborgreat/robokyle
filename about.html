How this started

Kyle is our friend. After his accident we started looking at adaptive equipment and found out what it costs. Some of us build things, so we started building what he needed instead and putting the files somewhere anyone could get them.

Why it is free

The hard part was rarely the material or the printing. It was knowing what to make and having a file to start from. Once that exists, copying it costs nobody anything, so charging for it never made sense to us.

Nothing on here is for sale. You do not need an account to look around or to download anything.

What this is not

Not a company, not a shop, not a startup. It is a small site, a few friends, and a pile of files that we would like to see get bigger. If something here looks rough, it is because we would rather publish a working design than polish it forever.

How you can help

  • Share a design you have actually built and used.
  • Take one that is close but not quite right, fix it, and post your version.
  • Answer a question in the comments if you know the answer.
  • Tell us what you need and cannot find. That is how most of these start.

Get in touch

Email robokyleorg@gmail.com, or open an issue on GitHub if you would rather do it that way.
