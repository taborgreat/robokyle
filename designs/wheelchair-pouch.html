Design

Wheelchair Side Pouch

A sewn pouch that straps under the armrest of most manual wheelchairs. Two zipped compartments, a magnetic phone slot on the outside, and a water-bottle sleeve on the inboard side. Pattern fits armrest tubing from 22mm to 32mm without modification.

Designed with three wheelchair users who were tired of everything either falling into their lap or being unreachable from the seat.

Working Sewing

What’s honest about the status

Pattern is stable at V3. It’s been through six sewists at three skill levels; each finished in under three hours.

How it works and why we designed it this way

A wheelchair user’s reachable zone is narrower than most people realise. Under the armrest is the one spot that’s always in easy reach, never blocks the wheel, and doesn’t snag on doorways. Every dimension in the pattern comes from that constraint.

The webbing wraps the armrest tube and closes with a side-release buckle, so the pouch can be removed one-handed at the end of the day — essential for wash cycles and for switching between multiple chairs.

Why magnets for the phone slot?

Zippers on a phone slot mean two hands. A magnetic flap is one hand, one motion. Testers were unanimous on this in the very first co-design session.

What we got wrong, and what we would change

  • V1 used Velcro for the strap closure. Loud, snagged on clothes, wore out fast. V2 switched to a buckle.
  • The water-bottle sleeve was 3" tall in V1 — too short to hold a bottle upright. V2 raised it to 5" and added a shock-cord cinch.
  • We didn’t include a fabric-choice guide in V1. New sewists picked slippery satins and the whole thing sagged. V3 ships a fabric-selection sheet.
