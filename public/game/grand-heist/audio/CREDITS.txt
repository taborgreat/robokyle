--- composed cues -------------------------------------------------

Weapon reloads and the "forcing a machine" grind are not separate
recordings. There is no CC0 reload recording on OpenGameArt — the
closest matches (Metal Click, Light Machine Gun) are CC-BY 4.0, which
this project does not use. They are instead layered from the CC0
samples above (metal_hit, hit_armor, glass_break, drill_bit) with a
different rhythm and pitch per weapon class:

    pistol   two clacks
    shotgun  three shells, then the pump
    rifle    magazine out, magazine in, bolt
    heavy    a belt, slowly
    rocket   tube open, round in, tube shut
    energy   cell swap and a spin-up

The machine-cracking grind pitches up as the lock gives way.
All source material remains CC0 1.0.
