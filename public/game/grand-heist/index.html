How to play

The loop

Pick a bank, pick three crew, spend your cash in the Armory, then pull the job. Money you carry out is both your score and your budget for the next one.

Things worth knowing

  • Melee is silent. Guns are not. You can knife an isolated guard before anyone hits the alarm.
  • The alarm starts the clock. Police arrive when it runs out, set up outside, then breach if you linger.
  • Carry capacity is the cap on greed. Bigger bags and the Carry stat decide how much you can actually take.
  • Crew death is permanent. They drop their cash where they fall — grab it before you leave, or it is gone.
  • Their gear is not lost. Equipment returns to the Armory as long as you extract.
  • RoboKyle cannot permanently die. He gets one self-revive per job; after that a crew member has to reach him.
