How to Play

The basics. Controls switch automatically depending on whether you’re on a keyboard or a touchscreen.

Keyboard & mouse

WASD Move (arrow keys work too)
Mouse Aim
Click Shoot, hold for autos
1 2 3 4 Swap weapon
Shift Sprint (short burst)
Scroll Zoom in / out
P or Esc Pause

Touchscreen

Left stick Move
Right stick Aim, fires while held
RUN Sprint (short burst)
1–4 Swap weapon
+ − Zoom in / out
II Pause

Enemies drop ammo. Rockets are rare. Waves get faster and meaner, so keep moving, back into open ground, and don’t let two grunts corner you.
