Contribute a design

Made something that works?

If you’ve built an adaptation that solves a real problem, we’d love to publish it here — with your name on it, under the license you pick. Files, a parts list, and a paragraph on who it helps is enough to start.

  • You keep authorship and copyright. We just host and format.
  • We’ll help write the tutorial and take the build photos if you want.
  • Every published design gets a plain-language review before it ships.
