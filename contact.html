The best way to reach us depends on why.

What to expect

Honest response times.

We’re volunteer-led, so we don’t promise 24-hour turnarounds. What we do promise: a real person reads every message, and you’ll hear back within two working days — even if the answer is "we can’t help with this one."

  • Every email lands with a human, not a triage bot.
  • GitHub issues get a first response within three working days.
  • Tester applications get a reply within a week, either way.
  • Nothing you send us gets added to a marketing list. Ever.
